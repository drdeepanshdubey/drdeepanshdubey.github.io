01 / About
Profile

A pharmaceutical scientist who works across the whole evidence chain.

I build the connective tissue between formulation science and the economic case that justifies a medicine.

I am a B.Pharm graduate (2026, First Honours Division) of the Shri Ram Group of Institutions, Jabalpur, now pursuing an M.Pharm in Pharmacology at GGITS. My specialization is computational pharmaceutics, with electives in regulatory science and pharmacovigilance. My research moves between in-silico formulation and PBPK modelling, AI-assisted drug repurposing, health-economic modelling — and, more recently, building AI agents for pharma. In 2026 I was selected as one of four trainees for a one-month hands-on PBPK modelling programme at NIPER Mohali, and I now teach what I learn through two 30-day PBPK series on LinkedIn.

The thread across all of it is rigour applied where access is decided. A formulation only matters if it is manufacturable; an outcome only matters if a payer will fund it. I work on both ends — designing carrier systems at the bench, and modelling cost-effectiveness, ICERs, and value-based pricing for the therapies that result.

I am a Registered Pharmacist (M.P. State Pharmacy Council) and open to roles and collaborations in computational pharmaceutics, HEOR and real-world evidence, regulatory affairs, or pharmaceutical data science — ideally where rural and frontier research can be brought closer together.
